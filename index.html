Privacy Policy

Blackout — Power Station
Effective 8 November 2026
Last Pixel Games develops and operates Blackout — Power Station. Contact gameslastpixel@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Game data on your device

Blackout stores progress, unfinished boards, settings, records and earned Bulb balances in the app's local storage. Core missions work offline and there are no player accounts or automatic gameplay analytics uploads. The app includes a Google AdMob integration. Advertising availability depends on the release configuration, network and service availability. This release has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or paid content.

Advertising

When enabled, Google AdMob supplies optional rewarded videos and automatic ads shown only after completed puzzles. Google and its partners may process IP address, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver, measure and protect advertising, according to their policies. Where applicable, the app requests consent through Google's consent form before requesting ads. Advertising options can be reopened from Settings. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . Google advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads .

Payments and purchase verification are disabled in this release. The game does not collect payment details or send purchase tokens to a purchase-verification service.

Optional playtest recording

Recording is off by default. If you enable it in the playtest build, the game stores a local log of sessions, board attempts, time and move limits, assist offers and use, Bulb balances and cosmetic feedback. It includes the app version, content and economy versions, event identifiers and locally generated session and attempt identifiers so events can be interpreted together. These identifiers are generated by the game and are not advertising or hardware identifiers.

The log stays on your device. It is limited to 256 KiB. Recording stops adding events when the file is full. Turning recording off stops new entries. Clear Playtest Log deletes the existing file.

Information you choose to share

Copy Playtest Log places the log on your device's clipboard. The game does not send it to us. If you choose to email a log, feedback or a support request, Last Pixel Games receives your email address and the information you include. A prepared support email includes the game version and operating-system details, which you can review and edit before sending. Your email provider handles the message under its own privacy policy.

We use information you send to respond to support requests and improve game reliability, difficulty and the Bulb economy. We do not sell it or use it for targeted advertising. We may disclose information when required by law.

Storage, retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Local playtest logs remain until you clear them, clear app data or uninstall the game. Android cloud backup is disabled in this build. Reinstalling the game or moving to another device does not restore local progress or earned Bulbs.

We retain support correspondence and playtest material you send while needed to handle your request, investigate an issue or evaluate the playtest, and as required by law. To request access, correction or deletion of information you sent us, email gameslastpixel@gmail.com from the address used to contact us. Copies you share with other people or services are controlled by their recipients.

Permissions and children

The game uses internet access for enabled ads and consent, and the vibration permission for optional haptic feedback. It does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone or location. Children should ask a parent or guardian before emailing us or sharing playtest material. If you believe a child sent personal information that should be removed, contact us.

Policy changes

We will update this policy when the game's data practices change. The effective date identifies the current version. Release disclosures and Google Play Data safety information must reflect the advertising, billing and hosting configuration used in that release.
